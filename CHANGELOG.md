# Changelog

All notable changes to **Beyond the LLM Boundary: Structural Hybrid Intelligence, Representation Barriers, and the Future of AI Architecture (BLB-SHI)** are documented here.

This project follows the principles of [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and uses semantic-style version labels for research releases. A version number identifies a published research snapshot; it does **not** imply that the proposed architecture has been implemented or empirically validated.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-27

### Added — Foundational Research Series

Eight conceptual research papers establishing the BLB-SHI research framework:

1. [BLB-001 — Language Intelligence and the Limits of Universal Coverage](docs/BLB-001-Language-Intelligence-and-the-Limits-of-Universal-Coverage.md): language-accessible intelligence, levels of expressibility, and coverage versus sufficiency.
2. [BLB-002 — Computational Representation Barriers Beyond Natural Language](docs/BLB-002-Computational-Representation-Barriers-Beyond-Natural-Language.md): representation-translation costs, operational equivalence, and representation-aware evaluation.
3. [BLB-003 — The Economics of LLM Scaling and Structural Specialization](docs/BLB-003-The-Economics-of-LLM-Scaling-and-Structural-Specialization.md): marginal intelligence economics, specialization break-even, and total lifecycle costs.
4. [BLB-004 — Perspective Sovereignty, Ownership, and the All-in-One Boundary](docs/BLB-004-Perspective-Sovereignty-Ownership-and-the-All-in-One-Boundary.md): perspective sovereignty, context ownership, sovereign brain units, and policy constraints.
5. [BLB-005 — Decoupling Structural Localization from Per-Node Intelligence](docs/BLB-005-Decoupling-Structural-Localization-from-Per-Node-Intelligence.md): localization–execution decoupling, node contracts, and hybrid execution.
6. [BLB-006 — CCC-Native Intelligence and Structural Semantic Units](docs/BLB-006-CCC-Native-Intelligence-and-Structural-Semantic-Units.md): structural semantic units, progressive CCC integration, and governed updates.
7. [BLB-007 — BASHI: Boundary-Aware Structural Hybrid Intelligence Architecture](docs/BLB-007-BASHI-Boundary-Aware-Structural-Hybrid-Intelligence-Architecture.md): unified reference architecture, boundary profiling, policy control, and verification.
8. [BLB-008 — From LLM Boundaries to Open-Ended Structural Intelligence Growth](docs/BLB-008-From-LLM-Boundaries-to-Open-Ended-Structural-Intelligence-Growth.md): structural growth operators, collective learning, evidence-gated promotion, and rollback.

### Added — Conceptual Framework

- The distinction **intelligence coverage ≠ representation sufficiency ≠ computational optimality ≠ governance adequacy**.
- The principle **universal interface ≠ universal internal architecture**.
- A six-axis boundary profile covering representation, computation, learning, perspective, ownership, and governance.
- **BASHI** (Boundary-Aware Structural Hybrid Intelligence) as a proposed, modular reference architecture.
- **Localization–Execution Decoupling** for locating appropriate structures before selecting per-node execution methods.
- **CCC-native intelligence** as a staged research direction, not a demonstrated model capability.
- **Perspective sovereignty** and **ownership partitioning** as distinct from perspective simulation.
- Evidence- and authorization-gated structural growth, including counter-evidence and rollback.

### Research Status and Limitations

- **Status:** conceptual research and architectural proposals; no claim of production deployment, benchmark superiority, or empirical confirmation.
- Quantitative illustrations in the papers, including any illustrative language-coverage percentages, are hypotheses rather than measured population-level findings.
- Proposed performance, economic, and governance benefits require matched baselines, explicit measurement protocols, and counter-evidence.
- The project is not a claim that natural language cannot encode formal structures, or that specialized systems always outperform general-purpose LLMs.
- No executable demonstration or benchmark suite is included in the initial research release.

## Release and Maintenance Conventions

- **Added:** new research papers, diagrams, interfaces, or documentation.
- **Changed:** substantive revisions to definitions, assumptions, or architecture.
- **Fixed:** errors, broken links, incorrect references, or inconsistent terminology.
- **Deprecated:** constructs retained temporarily but no longer recommended.
- **Removed:** withdrawn constructs or documents.
- **Security:** authorization, ownership, or policy-control issues with practical impact.

Future entries should distinguish **proposed**, **implemented**, **experimentally evaluated**, and **independently reproduced** results. Corrections that change a scientific claim should identify the affected paper and section.
